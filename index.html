About Me

I am a researcher through and through. I am interested in addressing complex problems and answering difficult questions using statistics. I completed my Ph.D. titled “A Comprehensive Approach to Cognitive Offloading” at Mississippi State University under the instruction of Dr. Julia Soares. My past research has focused on Memory, Comprehension, and Technology, but I have deeply generalizable research skills and am naturally curious about large scale issues such as broader psychology, economics, politics, and language learning. I am looking for challenging work at an institution in which I can contribute to social good.
